# RoboGrinder placement organizations

Track career placements, graduate study, PhD programs, and research destinations here. This list records organizations supplied by the team; member, role, program, and year details can be added below.

The marquee sorts names alphabetically and splits the active list into three consecutive groups, balanced as evenly as possible. The top and bottom rows move from right to left; the middle row moves from left to right. The current 40 organizations are split into 14, 13, and 13.

## University display cutoff

Working basis: the latest 2026 U.S. News overall graduate engineering or graduate computer science ranking, with ties at rank 10 included. Specialty, online, global, and overall university rankings do not count. This cutoff applies only to the public marquee; placement history remains in this tracker.

- Carnegie Mellon University: retained. [Official 2026 report](https://www.cmu.edu/news/stories/archives/2026/april/cmu-ranks-no-1-in-2026-us-news-graduate-rankings) lists graduate computer science at 1 and engineering at 10.
- Cornell University: retained for computer science. The [official 2026 Cornell Tech viewbook](https://tech.cornell.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Cornell-Tech-Viewbook-122025.pdf), page 44, reports Cornell at 7 in U.S. News graduate computer science for 2025. This is the confirmed ranking basis for this addition.
- Georgia Tech: retained. [Official 2026 report](https://news.gatech.edu/news/2026/04/07/georgia-tech-graduate-programs-stand-among-nations-best-2026-rankings) lists engineering at 4 and computer science at 7.
- UC Berkeley: retained. [Official 2026 report](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/news/2026/04/berkeley-engineering-ranked-third-best-grad-program-in-the-nation-by-u-s-news/) lists graduate engineering at 3.
- UC San Diego: retained. [Official rankings page](https://jacobsschool.ucsd.edu/about/rankings) lists graduate engineering at 9 in April 2026.
- Columbia University: recorded in the tracker, but not displayed because overall graduate engineering or computer science top 10 eligibility has not been confirmed.
- University of Michigan: retained for computer science. [Official rankings page](https://cse.engin.umich.edu/about/by-the-numbers/) lists institutional computer science at 10 for 2016 through 2026. Its overall graduate engineering rank is 12.
- Duke, Northeastern, UC Davis, Washington University in St. Louis, and USC: excluded from the public marquee under the overall graduate top 10 cutoff. All remain recorded above and below.

Ranking references: [U.S. News graduate engineering](https://www.usnews.com/best-graduate-schools/top-engineering-schools/eng-rankings), [U.S. News graduate computer science](https://www.usnews.com/best-graduate-schools/top-science-schools/computer-science-rankings), [Duke 2026 report](https://pratt.duke.edu/news/2026-us-news-graduate-rankings/), [UC Davis 2026 report](https://engineering.ucdavis.edu/news/uc-davis-engineering-graduate-ranking-2026), and [USC 2026 report](https://today.usc.edu/usc-graduate-programs/). Reviewed October 4, 2026.

## Updating the website

Edit `companies` in `src/content/industry-placements.json` or the Industry placements CMS collection. Entries include a name, unique slug, local logo path, original asset URL, and source page.

Download configured originals with `node scripts/sync-placement-logos.mjs`.

The marquee uses smooth grayscale with white mapped to #f0f0f0, black mapped to #595959, with a linear mapping for softer midtones. Artwork is framed using measured visible bounds, so transparent or white canvas padding does not determine spacing. Logos fit within 200px wide by 60px high on desktop, or 170px by 48px on mobile. Visible artwork has 72px horizontal gaps on desktop and 48px on mobile. Sponsor logos retain their full color.

`scripts/measure-placement-logos.mjs` regenerates `src/content/placement-logo-frames.json` without altering original files. The downloader runs this step automatically; run it separately after replacing local logos. SVG artwork preserves its aspect ratio inside measured frames, including Barclays. CAS uses the replacement wordmark supplied by the team.

Evoenergy uses the corrected source provided by the team. The original shared Ashby URL is a Dexmate wordmark and is assigned only to Dexmate.
